# Claim scope and limitations

## Claimed

- $r(7)=24$, for one-direction, one-lap rosaries exactly as defined by Gupta (1981) and Lecouturier–Zmiaikou (2012).
- As a by-product of the same search, $r(6)=17$.

## Not claimed

- No new upper bound: the 24-bead rosary was posted earlier (Ever-Hadani, 2026) and comes from the Lecouturier–Zmiaikou construction.
- Nothing about $n\ge 8$, the bidirectional variant, or Gupta's conjecture for general $n$.
- No new proof technique beyond the circular gap criterion as a stated lemma; the deletion argument and canonical augmentation are standard.
- No formal verification. The lower bound depends on an exhaustive computation, replicated by four separately written programs, all of which use the hand-proved reduction of paper §2.
- No peer review. The mathematical audits were performed by AI systems.
- Novelty is stated as "appears to be new after a documented search through 25 November 2026", with the source gaps listed in `STATUS.md`.
