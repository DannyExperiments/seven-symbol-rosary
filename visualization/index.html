Seven symbols, 24 beads

Can every ordering of 1–7 be read from beads on a circle, going once clockwise and skipping beads freely? This circle of 24 beads does it, and no circle of 23 beads can. Drag to rotate.

Pick an ordering of 1–7

The rising path is the reading route. It starts at one bead and climbs as it goes clockwise; it never completes a full turn, so no bead is used twice. Click a bead to remove it and see that every one of the 24 beads is needed.

Insert symbol 7 into a 6-symbol circle

Delete the three 7s and 21 beads remain. Green gaps are those from which P can be read in one lap. Reading “7, then P” works exactly when a 7 sits in a green gap, and every ordering of 1–7 is a rotation of some “7, then P”. So the 7s must touch the green gaps of all 720 orderings P. Click gaps to move the 7s and watch the count.
